## 1. Executive Summary & Scientific Justification

In release `v1.5.0-FOUR-CRITERION-FREEZE`, the subjective Literature Evidence Score ($s_{\text{lit}}$) was **permanently removed** from the active computational decision framework.

### Scientific Rationale
The computational framework does not independently execute automated literature searches, bibliographic quality scoring, or evidence grading for newly entered polymers. In previous iterations ($v1.4.0$), user-supplied or default literature scores were incorporated directly into the multi-criteria score matrix $\mathbf{S}$. This introduced a subjective, uncomputable ranking factor into an otherwise rigorous thermodynamic and molecular model.

To maintain strict scientific integrity and ensure that all current and future user-added polymers are evaluated on an identical, objective, and fully reproducible basis, literature/source information is now treated exclusively as **provenance metadata** and is completely excluded from active mathematical ranking.

## 2. Active Feature Space Architecture

### Historical v1.4.0 (5 Criteria — Superseded)
$$\mathbf{S}_{v1.4.0} = [s_{\text{HSP}},\; s_\chi,\; s_{\text{desc}},\; s_{\text{GT}},\; s_{\text{lit}}] \in [0, 1]^{N \times 5}$$

### Authoritative v1.5.0 (4 Criteria — Active Frozen Baseline)
$$\mathbf{S}_{v1.5.0} = [s_{\text{HSP}},\; s_\chi,\; s_{\text{desc}},\; s_{\text{GT}}] \in [0, 1]^{N \times 4}$$

| Criterion | Definition | Underlying Equation / Theory | Domain / Bounds |
|---|---|---|---|
| $s_{\text{HSP}}$ | Hansen Solubility Parameter distance affinity score | $s_{\text{HSP}} = \max(0,\; 1 - R_a / (2 R_0))$ | $[0, 1]$ |
| $s_\chi$ | Flory–Huggins interaction parameter compatibility score | $s_\chi = \max(0,\; 1 - \chi_{\text{Lindvig}})$ | $[0, 1]$ |
| $s_{\text{desc}}$ | 2D molecular descriptor similarity score | Weighted match across HBD, HBA, TPSA, aromaticity | $[0, 1]$ |
| $s_{\text{GT}}$ | Gordon–Taylor anti-plasticization glass stability score | $s_{\text{GT}} = \text{clip}\left(\frac{T_{g,\text{mix}} - (T_{g,\text{drug}} + 30)}{50},\; 0,\; 1\right)$ | $[0, 1]$ |

### 2D Descriptor ($s_{\text{desc}}$) Invariance Limitation
The descriptor score is invariant across the current five-polymer reference set ($s_{\text{desc}} = 0.2268$) and therefore contributes no variance to PCA discrimination. It remains structurally retained as a generalizable computational decision criterion for future candidate libraries.

## 3. Policy A: PCA Dimensionality & Monte Carlo K-Handling Policy

### Investigation of Stochastic Dimensionality
In the baseline 5-polymer research library, $s_{\text{desc}} = 0.2268$ across all 5 candidates due to monomer functional-group matching, yielding zero empirical variance in this specific reference set. Consequently, deterministic PCA retains $K=2$ principal components explaining **100.0%** cumulative variance.

### Explicit Framework Policy: Policy A (Input Perturbation with Fixed Baseline Decision Subspace)
**Policy Rule**: During Monte Carlo uncertainty quantification and sensitivity analysis, candidate realization vectors $\mathbf{S}_{\text{sim}} \in \mathbb{R}^{N \times 4}$ are **projected onto the established baseline PCA decision subspace**:
$$\mathbf{T}_{\text{sim}} = \text{StandardScaler}(\mathbf{S}_{\text{sim}}) \cdot \mathbf{P}_{\text{baseline}} \in \mathbb{R}^{N \times K_{\text{baseline}}}$$

**Scientific Rationale & Terminology**:
1. **Decision-Space Uncertainty Propagation**: Decision-space uncertainty propagation under the specified perturbation distributions and fixed baseline PCA decision subspace. This is not direct physical measurement-error propagation.
2. **Definition of $P(\text{top-1})$**: $P(\text{top-1})$ represents the probability that a candidate achieves rank #1 under the specified computational uncertainty perturbations. It is not a probability of experimental success.
3. **Semantic Axis Invariance**: Guarantees that PC1 consistently represents Thermodynamic Affinity and PC2 consistently represents Glass Stabilization across all 10,000 simulations without arbitrary eigenvector sign-flips or axis swaps.
4. **Exact AHP Alignment**: The established expert AHP weight vector $\mathbf{w}_{\text{baseline}} = [0.6667, 0.3333]$ applies directly to the projected coordinates without ad-hoc padding or truncation.
